Create a clean HD 2D game FX spritesheet for a compact hit spark damage impact.

OUTPUT FORMAT
- Exactly 6 animation frames in a 2 rows x 3 columns grid.
- Each cell is exactly 512 x 512 px, total sheet exactly 1536 x 1024 px.
- Use a solid flat #FF00FF background in every cell.
- Keep all visible effect pixels at least 86 px inside each cell edge.
- No labels, no text, no frame numbers, no borders, no UI.

FX SUBJECT
- Body-free impact spark effect only.
- No character, no weapon, no shield, no hand, no face, no armor.
- A compact melee hit spark that can layer over a torso when an actor takes damage.
- Shape: tight starburst and shard flash, about 250 px wide and 210 px tall at peak.
- Materials: bright white-yellow core, warm orange sparks, small charcoal ash flecks, a few short angular ember shards.
- Style: clean painterly HD fantasy game VFX, readable at combat scale, transparent-ready edges.

ANIMATION SEQUENCE
- Frame 1: tiny pre-impact glint, very compact.
- Frame 2: sharp white-yellow flash blooms.
- Frame 3: strongest impact, angular sparks radiate outward but stay compact.
- Frame 4: orange ember fragments trail off.
- Frame 5: ash flecks and fading glow.
- Frame 6: almost gone, loop-safe fade.

STRICT RULES
- Do not include a long slash arc, sword trail, fireball, smoke cloud, explosion plume, UI icon, or circular magic aura.
- Do not fill the whole cell; keep the effect tight and centered.
- Keep the same center anchor in all frames.
- Do not touch cell edges.
- Do not use a black background; the only background color must be solid #FF00FF.
