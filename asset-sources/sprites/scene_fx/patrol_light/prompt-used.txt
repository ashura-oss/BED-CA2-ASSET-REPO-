STRICT 2x2 looping sprite sheet for a warm gold patrol lantern/searchlight glow in a dark fantasy strategy game.

Canvas and layout:
- Exactly four animation frames arranged in a 2 columns by 2 rows grid.
- Each cell is one centered horizontal oval glow, no visible grid lines, no labels, no text.
- The glow should pulse from narrow to wider and back so it loops cleanly.
- Keep large empty margins around each glow so runtime scaling never clips on phone, laptop, or desktop canvases.

Art direction:
- Warm white core with amber and muted orange falloff.
- Soft smoky edge, readable on a dark Hearthvale night backdrop.
- No object body, no lantern hardware, no character, no scenery.

Chroma-key rule:
- Background must be solid flat #FF00FF magenta only, with no texture, no vignette, and no shadow.
- The glow must not touch the outer image edge or any cell edge.

Accepted postprocess:
- Original generated source preserved as raw-sheet-generated-original.webp.
- Normalized to a 1024x1024 2x2 source grid as raw-sheet.webp.
- Chroma cleanup used border-sampled soft matte and despill:
  remove_chroma_key.py --auto-key border --soft-matte --transparent-threshold 24 --opaque-threshold 118 --edge-feather 0.3 --despill
- Sprite processor:
  generate2dsprite.py process --target asset --mode patrol_light --rows 2 --cols 2 --cell-size 512 --label-prefix scene-patrol-light --fit-scale 0.86 --component-mode all --duration 150 --threshold 120 --edge-threshold 190

QC result:
- 4 frames, 512x512 each, composed into sheet-transparent.webp.
- Zero hot-magenta, pinkish, or purpleish residue by pixel scan.
- Zero opaque or partial-alpha pixels on frame edges.
